Kinesis Coach

Your AI coach

The Kinesis app shows every punch as you throw it. After each session it tells you what to work on next, and gives you drills, lessons and plans made from your own data.

01

Pads

A voice calls out combos and you throw them back. The app checks every punch and scores how fast you were and how quickly you reacted.

02

Shadow, Spar and Tests

Timed rounds where every punch is counted and named. Tests measure your top speed, your reaction time and how many punches you can throw.

03

Learn

Short lessons, one punch at a time. The app checks every rep and names the mistake, for example "hand came back low", with the fix.

04

Programs

Four-week plans with a retest every week, so you can see if you are really getting faster.

05

Patterns

The app looks across all your sessions for habits, like hardly ever throwing an uppercut or slowing down in later rounds.

06

AI coach

Ask it anything about your training. It writes a short debrief after each session and can plan your week from your data.

07

Live 3D figure

A figure on screen copies your arm while you train, so you can see your punches as you throw them.

08

Voice control

Say "Kinesis, start" to begin a session without touching your phone.
